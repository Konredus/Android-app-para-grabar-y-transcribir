Verbapp was born for your SECOND BRAIN. What you say becomes notes ready to keep: record meetings, ideas and conversations offline and, whenever you want, transcribe them with AI, with every speaker by name and a note that goes straight to your Obsidian vault or the folder you choose.

BUILT FOR YOUR SECOND BRAIN
• One AI note per recording: summary, decisions, tasks and key quotes, in Markdown (.md) with properties and tags.
• Save it with one tap to your Obsidian vault (for example your 0-Inbox), to Google Drive or to any folder you use.
• Plus the full text (.txt) with who said what, so you can search and link it whenever you need it.

RECORD WITH ONE TAP
• Works offline: your recordings stay on your phone.
• Keeps recording with the screen locked.
• Mark key moments with ★ while you record, even from the notification.
• Import audio from WhatsApp, voice recorders and other apps (M4A, MP3, WAV and more).

TRANSCRIBE AND SEPARATE SPEAKERS
• Transcribe with the latest speech-to-text models through OpenRouter. The list updates itself and “Automatic” picks the recommended one.
• Separates each person who speaks and recognizes the voices you already know: yours and those of the people you talk to most.
• Fix who said what with one tap, always with Undo.
• Long recordings are split into parts that are sent in parallel.

YOUR METRICS
• How much you talk, your streaks, when you record and who you talk with.
• How much you’ve spent on transcriptions, with the real cost reported by OpenRouter.
• Everything is calculated on your phone.

YOU CONTROL YOUR DATA
• No account, no ads and no tracking.
• Audio leaves your phone only when you ask for a transcription (or turn on automatic transcription), and it goes to OpenRouter with your own key.
• Send on Wi-Fi only, or use mobile data for a single recording when you need to.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
• English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and German. Verbapp starts in your phone’s language, and you can change it anytime.

HOW TO START
Recording is free and needs no setup. To transcribe you need an OpenRouter key (openrouter.ai): with a single key you transcribe and create your notes, and you pay OpenRouter only for what you use, with no subscription. Depending on the model you choose, an hour of audio costs from about US$0.10.

Made for people who build a second brain and talk more than they type: meetings, classes, interviews, voice notes and conversations.
